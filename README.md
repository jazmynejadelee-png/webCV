# CV Professional Profile Generator  

## Purpose 
- working history, skills and experiences with job descriptions and more content

## Inputs 
- `Career history`: past roles and responsibilities.
- `target role`: specifics job being applied for and industry.
- `described role`: descriptions of past roles.


## Output Contract  
- **Length:**
- **Structure:** 
- *main roles 1: * Seasonal working such as packing, picking and grafting thinning etc.  
- *Secondary role 2: * Commercial cleaning, schools, businesses and offices 
- *work roles 3: * Forestry runner, and floater 


## Guardrails  
- *Work values. Professional values while working in and around site.:*
- *Safe working environment*
- *evacuation methods*


## Examples 
- **Input 1:* 4-5 Years of field work in horticulture and agriculture picking, packing fruit and veggies for different companies.
- **Input 2: * 2 years of cleaning experience in commercial cleaning and personal house cleaning.
- **Input 3: * 1 year of forestry as a runner then floater for river sun and coxco.
- **Expected Output:* Gaining as much experience and mahi under the circumstances learning skills and knowledge of horticulture and agriculture.
